Context

Paid social needs a business objective, not just an ad account.

This case study is designed to document one of Jose Paolo Olan’s freelance Meta media buying engagements once the client, campaign period, objective and data are confirmed. The current portfolio does not invent spend, CPL, CPA, ROAS or revenue.

Before publishing client data: remove account IDs and customer information, anonymize confidential client details where required, and use only metrics that can be verified from Ads Manager, CRM, analytics or approved reporting.
My Role

Hands-on campaign management and optimization.

  • Support campaign setup and structure in Meta Ads Manager.
  • Define or refine audience targeting based on campaign objectives and client context.
  • Monitor budgets, delivery and core performance indicators.
  • Review creative performance and identify testing opportunities.
  • Adjust targeting, budget allocation and campaign elements when performance signals justify a change.
  • Translate campaign performance into practical client recommendations.
