Context

Coordinating social content across a multi-centre portfolio.

The role involved supporting organic social initiatives across approximately 15 INTO centres across US, UK and Asia markets. The work required balancing centre-level content needs with platform requirements, campaign priorities, brand consistency and publishing workflows.

My Role

Content execution, workflow coordination and performance review.

  • Managed and supported organic social initiatives across Facebook and Instagram.
  • Developed platform-specific social copy and creative assets.
  • Coordinated content calendars and publishing workflows with marketing and stakeholder teams.
  • Monitored content performance using GA4 and Meta Business Suite.
  • Used observed performance signals to inform future creative and content decisions.

Measurement

Separate organic performance from paid amplification.

A representative Newcastle University Facebook snapshot from 15 Oct 2025 to 25 Sep 2026 shows why attribution discipline matters. The account recorded 867,934 total views, of which 25,153 were organic and 842,781 came from ads. Organic views were up 6.2% versus the comparison period.

Measurement principle: total account performance is useful context, but paid-driven totals should not be presented as organic impact. For this case study, organic-specific figures are identified separately from account-wide metrics.
Evidence

Current evidence snapshot: Newcastle University.

The first evidence set now includes Meta Business Suite content and results views for a representative centre. More centres will be added as the evidence library grows.

Content overview

The reviewed snapshot shows a mix of short-form and static content, including 35 Reels, 24 Photos and 3 Stories. The top-content panel includes student-led and student-experience themes such as centre tours and practical “what I wish I knew” content.

Results view

The account-level results view records 364.0K viewers, 3.3K link clicks, 7.2K visits, 1.4K content interactions and 185 follows during the displayed period. These are account totals, not organic-only metrics.
