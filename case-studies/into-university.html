Context

Coordinating social content across multiple markets.

The role involved supporting organic social initiatives across Facebook and Instagram for US, UK and Asia markets, aligning content with campaign and brand objectives while adapting execution to platform needs.

My Role

Content execution, workflow coordination and performance review.

  • Managed and supported organic social initiatives across Facebook and Instagram.
  • Developed platform-specific social copy and creative assets.
  • Coordinated content calendars and publishing workflows with marketing and stakeholder teams.
  • Monitored content performance using GA4 and Meta Business Suite.
  • Used observed performance insights to improve future creative and content decisions.
