Context

Coordinating social content across a multi-centre portfolio.

The role involved supporting organic social initiatives across approximately 15 INTO centres in US, UK and Asia markets. The work required balancing centre-level content needs with platform requirements, campaign priorities, brand consistency and publishing workflows.

My Role

Content execution, workflow coordination and performance review.

  • Managed and supported organic social initiatives across Facebook and Instagram.
  • Developed platform-specific social copy and creative assets.
  • Coordinated content calendars and publishing workflows with marketing and stakeholder teams.
  • Monitored content performance using GA4 and Meta Business Suite.
  • Used observed performance signals to inform future creative and content decisions.

Measurement

Performance-informed without exposing internal reporting.

GA4 and Meta Business Suite were used internally to monitor content performance, identify patterns and inform future content choices. To keep this public portfolio lower-risk, internal account screenshots, campaign exports, audience data, budgets and exact internal performance figures are intentionally not published here.

Portfolio approach: show the scale of the role, the operating process and public-facing work, while keeping confidential reporting and proprietary account data out of the public site.
