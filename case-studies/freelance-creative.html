Context

Translating business needs into usable digital creative.

Freelance projects required adapting to different brand identities, industries, promotional objectives and delivery timelines while keeping output suitable for the intended digital channel.

My Role

Creative execution with client and platform context.

  • Partnered with clients across e-commerce and service industries.
  • Developed social content, branded graphics, promotional creatives and other digital marketing assets.
  • Translated client objectives and brand identity into campaign concepts and platform-specific executions.
  • Produced social posts, advertising creatives, banners, logos and promotional materials.
  • Managed multiple client projects while meeting agreed timelines and brand requirements.
